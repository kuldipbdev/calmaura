How to Meditate in 10 Minutes

Simple steps to create a calm practice even on your busiest days.

1. Sit comfortably

Choose a quiet spot and settle into a position that feels steady and relaxed.

2. Breathe naturally

Let your breath flow without forcing it. Notice the feeling of the inhale and exhale.

3. Focus gently

Return your attention to your breath whenever your mind wanders.

4. Close with gratitude

End with a few moments of appreciation for your body and mind.
