Morning Meditation for Focus

Ease into the day with a grounding practice that supports concentration.

Set an intention

Choose one word for the day such as focus, calm, or clarity.

Take three slow breaths

Allow your shoulders to soften and your jaw to relax.

Notice the present moment

Use the next few minutes to anchor yourself before the day begins.
